good design is a moving target

2026-07-30

ai is making today’s “good design” cheap. once people can one-shot the same polished landing page, product, or piece of packaging, that aesthetic becomes generic ai slop.

taste then moves on. good design will always sit slightly ahead of what models can produce by default. good design will always live a few months ahead of the models.
